## Design Context

### Users

Developers and small teams using Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, Cline and related
AI coding tools. They open Agent Doctor after a task feels unexpectedly slow,
expensive or unreliable, and need a fast, credible explanation plus a safe next
step without sharing prompts, source files or credentials.

### Brand Personality

Evidence-first, capable, and calm. The product should feel like a trusted local
instrument panel: precise enough for experienced engineers, yet direct enough
for a first-time user to understand what is known, estimated, or unavailable.

### Aesthetic Direction

Editorial diagnostic console rather than a generic SaaS dashboard. Use an
ink-and-paper base with a deep evergreen operational accent and restrained
signal colors. Prefer asymmetric information blocks, data-led typography, and
purposeful disclosure over glass panels, neon gradients, or decorative charts.
Support both light and dark themes with clear contrast and reduced-motion
fallbacks.

### Design Principles

1. Every number shows its provenance and precision before it asks for trust.
2. Start with the next useful action; reveal dense evidence on demand.
3. Preserve calm hierarchy under long project names, missing data, and small
   screens; unavailable data is a real state, never a zero.
4. Local-only privacy must be visible but never interrupt primary work.
5. Motion explains transitions and state changes, and respects reduced-motion
   preferences.
